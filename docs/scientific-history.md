# Scientific history and guardrails

MRSPD evolved through a sequence of increasingly strict hypotheses. This file is intentionally explicit about negative results.

## Phase-surface stage

The original universal monotone-threshold hypothesis was not supported. Non-monotone spline models exposed horizon-dependent topology, but many mathematical roots were not statistically certifiable.

## Critical-horizon stage

A long-horizon R-only regime appeared in the discovery universe. Dense-grid refinement was treated as exploratory resolution refinement, not as a new independent confirmation.

## Frozen v8 independent validation

The frozen spectrum-wide R-only critical-horizon hypothesis failed to replicate. A forensic audit found the implementation invariants consistent and classified the result as statistical non-replication. The compact verdict is preserved in `results/reference/v8_independent_validation_forensic_summary.json`.

## Transport v9/v9.2

The project moved from a global root-free requirement to a percentile-normalized upper spectral tail and robust cross-sectional response. The U.K. universe supported the existence of a robust reversal tail at a later critical horizon, but the earlier fixed localization target was not confirmed.

## v10 cross-market hypothesis

The new hypothesis explicitly allowed `h_c` to depend on the market while freezing the tail coordinate (`q_psi >= 0.85`) and all certification rules before Japanese V4 construction.

The recorded Japanese V4 result identified both modes and passed the v10 confirmatory endpoint. This supports cross-market transport of the robust reversal-tail phenomenon; it does **not** establish one universal numerical `h_c`.
